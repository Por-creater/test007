Transformer — Self-Attention

For every token, self-attention computes a weight against every other token in the sequence — "how relevant is that word to understanding this one" — and builds the token's new representation as a weighted blend of every other token's value vector. No recurrence, no convolution: every position can look directly at every other position in one step.

hover or click a token to see what it attends to
full attention matrix (row = query token, column = key token)

Each row sums to 1 — it's a softmax over similarity scores between the query token's Q vector and every token's K vector. Brighter cells mean a larger share of that row's attention "budget" goes to that column.

Why this replaced recurrence

An RNN/LSTM has to pass information step by step through the sequence, so a dependency between token 1 and token 50 has to survive 49 hops. Self-attention connects any two positions directly, which is both easier to learn and trivially parallelizable across the whole sequence at once.

XGBoost — Gradient Boosting

Boosting builds an ensemble one small model at a time. Round 1 starts from a flat guess (the mean); every later round trains a shallow tree ("stump") to predict the residual error the ensemble still has, then adds a shrunk copy of that stump's prediction to the running total. Errors get chipped away round by round instead of all at once.

Each stump only needs to explain what's left over — a much easier job than fitting the whole function at once. The learning rate (shrinkage, here 0.4) keeps any single round from overcorrecting.

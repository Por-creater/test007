Reinforcement Learning — Q-Learning in a Grid World

There's no labeled dataset here — an agent takes actions, receives a reward, and slowly learns a Q-table: an estimate of "how good is this action from this state" for every state-action pair. It explores randomly at first (epsilon-greedy) and leans more on its own estimates as they improve.
